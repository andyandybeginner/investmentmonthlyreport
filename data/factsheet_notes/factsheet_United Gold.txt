Investment Objective
The Fund aims to achieve returns on investment mainly in securities of corporations
(whether or not listed on any stock exchange, and in any part of the world) whose
business (in any part of the world) is or is substantially in the mining or extraction of gold,
silver or precious metals, bulk commodities, base metals of all kinds, and other
commodities and it includes the mining or extraction of oil, gas, coal, alternative energy
or other commodities or other minerals.
