Investment Objective 
The investment objective of the Fund is to generate long term positive return, which include both 
capital appreciation and income. 
 
Investment Focus and Approach 
The Investment Manager seeks to achieve the objective of the Fund by investing primarily in equities, 
preferred shares, stock warrants, convertibles, cash and cash equivalents. 
The investment universe will include, but not limited to, equities and equities-related securities listed on 
exchanges globally. 
The Fund's investment in China "A" Shares listed on PRC Stock Exchanges may be made through the 
Stock Connects and/or other means as may be permitted by the relevant regulations from time to time, 
for up to 35% of the Fund's net asset value.  
  
The Investment manager may also make indirect investments in equities via other eligible access 
products (where the underlying assets would comprise equities defined above).

Market Review 
Global equity markets rose in August, with MSCI All Country World Index up 2.7% in USD terms. Best contributing regions were United States 
(+2.7%), Taiwan (+6.5%), and South Korea (+6.9%), while France (-2.0%), China (-0.5%), and United Kingdom (-0.5%) were the largest 
detractors. From a sector perspective, the best performing sectors were Information Technology (+6.0%), Materials (+10.4%), and Health 
Care (+3.9%), while worst performing sectors were Utilities (-3.1%), Consumer Staples (-1.2%), and Real Estate (-2.0%).  
  
In the US, following strong August 2026 employment data and ongoing inflation concerns, markets increased the probability of a 25-basis 
point hike in federal funds target range in September. Real GDP grew at an annualised rate of 1.5% in 2Q26, while the labour market 
strengthened in August: non-farm payroll employment increased by 162,000 and the unemployment rate held at 4.1%. Market remained 
concerned about the risks from rising capital expenditure in the technology sector.   
  
In the euro area, economic activity improved in 2Q26, with revised GDP up 0.6% quarter on quarter and 1.2% year on year. However, 
inflationary pressures rose in August, with harmonised index of consumer prices (HICP) increasing to 3.3% in August, from 2.9% in July, while 
energy inflation increased to 14.3% from 10.0%. Markets continued to assess the persistence of energy shock and its implications for the 
ECB's policy path.  
  
China's equity market edged lower in August despite some improvement in factory activity. Official manufacturing PMI rose to 49.8 in August 
from 49.2 in July but remained in contractionary area; non-manufacturing PMI held at 49.0. CPI rose 0.8% year on year, up from 0.5%, while 
PPI rose 3.8%, up from 3.5%. 
 
Investment Strategy 
We are positive on global equities on a 12-month basis. We continue to find compelling opportunities from our investment themes, including AI 
diffusion, disruption, and policy beneficiaries. AI adoption is proliferating across industries, creating new investment opportunities across 
sectors. 
  
The surge in computing demand for AI computing is creating manufacturing bottlenecks, which is supporting supply-chain companies to see 
strong earnings growth. However, we are also mindful that not all companies will be able to sustain such strong returns. We are finding 
selected opportunities in technology, financials, industrials, healthcare, defence, and basic materials sectors. 
  
In the US, we believe capital investment in the industrial and manufacturing sectors should benefit leading companies with strong market 
positions. We are also seeing opportunities in the financial sector, which may benefit from higher interest rates and growth in wealth 
management. 
  
In Asia, we remain positive on leading suppliers in the manufacturing supply chain. We also expect China to continue investing in technology 
and AI, as it seeks to achieve AI self-sufficiency. China's renewable energy solutions could also benefit from higher demand amid geopolitical 
uncertainty.
